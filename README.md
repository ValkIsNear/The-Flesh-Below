# Echoes of the Deep

Napravi mi igricu koja je nalik signalisu, poprilicno je kratka i zavrsava se u nekih 15 minuta, zelim da ima tuznu pricu i da bude stil u pikselima. Isto tako kao sto se u signalisu koristi nemacki kao govorni jezik za fraze ovde ce tako da se koristi srpski. I also want to have lesbians. Ovo je worldbuilding koji za sada imas od koga zelim da mi napravis prototip.
Evo kako cijeli tvoj koncept možemo prevesti i uobličiti u profesionalni Game Design / Worldbuilding Pitch i profile likova na engleskom jeziku, spremno za koncept igre, zajedno sa strukturiranim početkom priče.

⚓ GAME OVERVIEW & WORLDBUILDING



Aesthetic Fusion: Signalis (bio-meat decay/existential dread) + Pressure (abyssal depth/claustrophobia) + KOG (uncanny biology/liminal wrongness) + Europabiz Spotify Playlist Vibe (melancholic Soviet-bloc post-punk and industrial synth-pop).

The Setting: The Heavy (The Sub-Strata)



The World: The surface is a dead, frozen wasteland. Humanity has crawled into The Sub-Strata, a colossal, flooded network of pressurized concrete tunnels, rusted Soviet-era machinery, and organic veins.

The Sea (The Vital Fluid): There is no water here. The "sea" is The Vital Fluid—a thick, dark, pressurized mixture of industrial runoff, saltwater, and liquified biomass that slowly "eats" the steel and concrete. The environment constantly pulses like an open wound.



The Purpose: The State sends modified human divers called Anglers into the abyss. They are not fishing for food; they are harvesting Organ-Clusters (Bio-Meat Tumors) that grow on submerged reactors. These living hearts and nervous systems power the dying cities above.



The Theme: The Heavy is a literal synonym for severe depression. Moving through it feels like wading through heavy, exhausting stagnation. It strips away your human nature ("narav") until you either become a machine or melt into the walls.

👤 CHARACTER PROFILES

1. TAMARA (Asset #77-T) – The Angler



Role: Deep-Trench Harvester / Solo Navigator.



Age: 25.



Visual Style (KOG / Signalis hybrid): * She is unnaturally thin with elongated limbs and extra-jointed fingers for repairing rusted electronics.

She wears a worn, dark beanie over her head , but her heavy diving helmet is grafted directly into her skull and cheekbones.

Unlike other Anglers who have their eyes replaced by green sonar sensors, Tamara still has her biological eyes. They are a prototype modification: Silver Chromatism—her eyes look like blank, pupil-less chrome mirrors that reflect the red alarms and black oil around her. Due to the 800 atmospheres of pressure, her sclera (whites of the eyes) are permanently ruptured, making her look like she is constantly crying blood.

Her right leg is a heavy, hydraulic prosthetic covered in dark, greasy rubber, bolted to her hip. When she walks on metal grates, it makes a distinct, disturbing sound: Klang-Tap. Klang-Tap.



Psychological Profile ("Narav"): Cold, detached, and deeply dissociated. She has explosive, repressed anger issues directed at the State and the machinery. Because she kept her eyes, she is an anomaly—the other blind Anglers fear her, touching her face with long fingers because they don't understand what "seeing" is. She pushes everyone away because she carries the ultimate grief.



The Prosthetic Reason: During a chaotic harvest, a massive tumor went into a reactive spasm and clamped onto her leg like a steel trap. The suit's safety release failed. To avoid being completely absorbed by the screaming bio-meat wall, Tamara used her own vibro-cutter to saw off her own leg to escape. She now uses the heavy metal peg-leg as a weapon and an anchor, stabbing it into the floor to hold her ground against the pulsing currents.



The Past Trauma: She had a girl she loved on the surface. When the State sentenced Tamara to the Lower World due to her "Genomic Debt" and her unstable anger , Tamara chose not to tell her the truth. She left without a goodbye, letting her love believe she was just reassigned, hiding the fact that nobody ever returns from the deep.

2. KAYLA / ISIDORA – The Watcher (The Airlock Controller)



Role: Operations Operator / Mission Control.



Visual Style: Pale, hollow, "icon-like" face with deep, dark circles (podočnjaci) under her eyes from staring at grainy green monitors for 48-hour shifts. She moves with mechanical, stop-motion efficiency.



Relationship to Tamara: She is "The Solid" to Tamara's "The Heavy". While Tamara fights the pressure with rage and holds onto memories like anchors, Kayla chose to completely suppress and erase her own past to remain sane and functional for the system. She acts cold, but she secretly protects Tamara. She watches Tamara’s radar dot like a literal lifeline, keeping her grounded so Tamara doesn't succumb to the madness below.

📖 HOW THE STORY BEGINS (The Opening Scene)

The story should start in media res (in the middle of the action) to immediately drop the reader into the claustrophobic, sensory terror of the world.

Chapter 1: The Pressure Check



The Setup: The story opens in the dim, red-tinted darkness of Airlock 4. Tamara is sitting alone on a rusted steel bench, the tinned, distorted sound of a melancholic Soviet synth-pop track echoing weakly from the station’s cracked overhead intercom.

Sensory Hook: She is adjusting her gear. The fluid inside her hydraulic right leg is cold, making it sluggish. She kicks her heel against the floor—Klang. Tap. Klang. The hollow sound echoes through the chamber, a reminder that she is part human, part machine, and entirely owned by the State. In her pocket rests a broken, unlit cigarette—a useless piece of the surface she treats like a relic.



The Metaphor Introduced: The air in the Sub-Strata doesn't feel like air; it feels like an overdue debt. Kayla's flat, static-heavy voice crackles over the comms: "Pressure at 800 atmospheres. Floodgates opening. Check your seals, Tamara."



The Inciting Incident (The Deep-Sea Incident): Tamara drops into the pitch-black Vital Fluid to do a solo maintenance sweep in a semi-abandoned sector. Her chrome eyes cut through the dark, filtering reality into a sickening, bruised-purple and neon-grey spectrum of flesh and iron.

The Uncanny Discovery: Inside a jammed drainage duct, she doesn't find mechanical debris. She finds a Husk—the semi-organic, discarded shell of a previous Angler whose body was integrated into the wall. But this Husk is holding a waterlogged, rusted cassette tape or a small object from the surface.

The Haunting Hook: When Tamara touches it, her deep dissociation shatters. The Husk's wire-and-meat fingers twitch. Through her prototype visor, the shifting, pulsing bio-meat on the Husk's face begins to morph, taking the shape of the girl she left behind on the surface. Kayla screams through the radio static: "Tamara, back away! It has no face! It's just meat and rust calibration! It's feeding on your grief!"



The Ending of Chapter 1: Tamara drops the object and backs away into the dark tunnel, but behind her, through the low-frequency industrial hum, she hears the wet, clicking sound of the bio-meat wall trying to mimic a human voice—learning how to speak her lost lover's name. The abyss has officially recognized her.
